## 3. Concept
- **Idea (≤ 8 words):** He builds shopfronts that actually earn.
- **Device:** one browser window (chrome, address bar, gold "live" dot). It never leaves: it navigates through the sites, becomes one tile of the wall, and the wall hands off to the avatar.
- **Grammar:** the window is the stage; the site changes *inside* it (page wipes up, URL rolls), never by a cut, until the wall.
- **Logline:** One browser window navigates seven real client sites, then pulls back to the whole wall. (15 words)
- **Personality:** warm, assured, precise → Poppins (brand-supplied) 600 for statements, 300 for the descriptor; sentence case; light cream stage (brand-supplied); 18 px window corners; motion unhurried and weighted.
- **Mark:** brand-supplied — the 3D avatar in a circle with the name beside it (no mark exploration: the portfolio already has its identity).
- **Accent:** `#D4A24C` gold (brand-supplied, amber family) means **"live"**: the dot in the address bar when a page has loaded, the tiles' live dots in the wall. Never text on cream. **Stage:** light cream throughout. **Luminance plan:** cream stage → the sites' own colour inside the window → a wall of colour → cream lockup.
- **Signature move:** the page wipe-up (a new page rising inside the window from its bottom edge): first load (open), each navigation (middle), the avatar disc rising in its circle at the lockup (close).
- **Easing characters:** `cam` (pull-backs, creep), `out` (page wipes, arrivals), `exit` (the one velocity-handoff ramp).
- **Sonic signature:** none (silent master).

## 4. Arc and device path
- **Arc:** hook 1 → turn (first load) 2–3 → proof (navigation through the sites) 4–9 → promise (the wall) 10–12 → lockup 13 + tail.
- **Device path:** blank window, tight on the address bar → types and accepts a domain → the page wipes up (signature) → pull-back to the full window → navigates (page wipes, URL rolls) ×6 → pull-back: it becomes one tile of the wall → the wall accelerates left (velocity handoff) → the avatar disc rises into its circle (signature, close) beside the name.
